Benchside

A hands-free build helper for your iPhone. Prop the phone at your workbench, say “Buddy, is this the right part?”, and it looks and answers.

Benchside can also draft a build plan from your photos, a video or a PDF of the instructions, keep notes as you go, and make a recap video of the build. Answers come from an AI service through your own API key. There is no account and no sign-in.

Support

Email hello@shimskylabs.com and we will help.

Where do I get an API key?
Benchside uses your own Anthropic API key. Create one at console.anthropic.com (the Connect screen in the app opens that page for you), then paste it into Benchside. Anthropic bills usage to your own account. It is separate from Benchside Pro.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open Settings in Benchside and tap “Restore Purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I delete my data?
Your data is on your phone, not with us. Deleting a project deletes its photos, clips, recap and instructions. Remove your API keys in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything else, except items saved in the iOS Keychain. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?
Apple handles purchases and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
