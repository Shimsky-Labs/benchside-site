Benchside Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Benchside is a camera assistant for people building physical projects. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What Benchside collects

Nothing. Benchside has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. The developer never receives your images, audio, questions or keys.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

  • Camera frames and your questions are sent to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) using the API key you provide, only when you ask a question. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy for API users.
  • If you enable Roboflow features, small camera frames are sent to the Roboflow inference endpoint you choose (by default Roboflow's hosted API) using the Roboflow API key you provide, and, if you turn on contribution, frames are uploaded to a Roboflow project in your own Roboflow account. Roboflow's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Nothing else. No third-party SDKs, no advertising, no tracking.

Microphone and speech

Speech is recognized on your device using Apple's speech framework when the device supports it. Only the text of a question you ask is sent to Anthropic. Audio is never recorded or uploaded.

Your API keys

Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you enable iCloud Keychain sync in the app, Apple syncs them to your other devices using Apple's encrypted keychain sync. Keys are sent only to the services they belong to.

Project data

Your projects, plans and running notes are stored on your device (and in your iCloud device backup if enabled). You can delete them in the app.

Children

Benchside is not directed at children under 13.
