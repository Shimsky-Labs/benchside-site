Benchside Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Who we are

Benchside is an iPhone app published by Shimsky Labs LLC, a company in the United States. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Shimsky Labs LLC.

Email: hello@shimskylabs.com
Postal address: 15 Round Hill Drive, Briarcliff Manor, NY 10510, USA

Benchside is a hands-free helper for building things at a bench. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • We collect very little. Benchside has an optional account, made with Sign in with Apple. If you make one, we keep a small record: your Apple sign-in ID, the email Apple shares (or Apple’s private relay address), when you joined and last opened the app, which Terms you agreed to and when, your app version, and whether Pro is on. Without an account we receive nothing. Either way, Benchside has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and your questions, pictures and projects never come to us.
  • AI answers use your own key. When you ask a question, the app sends pictures and your question straight from your phone to the AI service you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), using your own API key for it. Before the first send, the app names that company and asks your permission. It asks again, naming the new company, if you switch.
  • Everything else is optional. Roboflow, ElevenLabs and the downloadable voice are used only if you set them up yourself.
  • Your projects stay on your phone. Notes, photos, clips and recap videos are kept on the phone, and you can delete them.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

Everything below goes directly from your phone to the company named. Except the optional account record, nothing passes through us.

How the AI companies protect it. Anthropic and OpenAI, under their terms for API customers, do not use what you send to train their models by default, and keep it only for a limited time: Anthropic deletes it within 30 days unless its safety checks flag it, and OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Google gives the same protection only on its paid tier, so use a Gemini key from a Google Cloud project with billing turned on; on Google’s free tier, Google uses what you send to improve its products, and people may read it. With those settings, these companies protect your data at least as well as this policy does. ElevenLabs (the optional online voice) is different: under its own terms it may use the text it receives to train its AI models unless you opt out with ElevenLabs.

Your Benchside account (optional)

You can make an account with Sign in with Apple, during setup or later in Settings › Account. Everything in Benchside works without one.

What the account record holds: your Apple sign-in ID (a number Apple gives Benchside for you), the email Apple shares with us or Apple’s private relay address if you chose to hide your email, when you joined and when you last opened the app, which version of the Terms of Use you agreed to and when, the app version, and whether Benchside Pro is on. Nothing else: your questions, pictures, notes and projects are never part of it.

Who stores it: Google’s Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, in the United States) stores it for us. Like any sign-in service, Firebase sees your IP address and device type when you sign in. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

What we use it for: to know who uses Benchside, to keep a record of your agreement to the Terms, and to help you if you contact us. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

Deleting it: Settings › Account › Delete account deletes the account and its record straight away, and tells Apple to stop sharing your Apple ID with Benchside. You can also email us and we will delete it.

What stays on your phone

These are kept on your phone only. We cannot see them.

  • Your projects and notes.
  • The questions you asked and the answers you got, kept with each project so you can come back to them or carry on from an earlier point. They can be read only while the phone is unlocked and are left out of iCloud and computer backups. The app also keeps a short note about how you like to work (for example your experience and the units you use). You can read, change, erase or switch it off in Settings; it stays on your phone and is sent to the AI service with your questions.
  • While the camera is open: a photo of the bench about every 45 seconds, and a short silent clip of each question. These are for the build recap. A switch in Settings turns this off.
  • Instruction PDFs you added.
  • Recap videos the app has made, and videos you film for a recap.
  • Your API keys, in the iOS Keychain.
  • A count of the free questions you have used. It is in the iOS Keychain, so it is still there if you delete the app and install it again.

Saved conversations, questions and answers, recap photos, clips and recap videos are left out of iCloud and computer backups and can be read only while the phone is unlocked. Your projects, notes and PDFs can be included in your iPhone backups, like other apps’ files. Backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Second camera

You can pair another phone as a second camera by scanning a QR code. The pairing code is kept in the Keychain on each phone and is never sent over the internet. Its pictures are streamed over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, to the paired phone only. When your recap switch is on, the main phone also keeps those pictures for the recap. They are included with the questions you send to the AI service.

Microphone and speech

  • Benchside uses Apple’s speech framework to turn what you say into text. This happens on your device when your device supports it. Otherwise Apple processes the audio.
  • Benchside never stores or uploads the audio it hears.
  • With hands-free on, the microphone listens while the app is open. Only what you say to the assistant is sent to the AI service, and it is sent as text. That means what follows its name, a clear question even without the name (speech recognition often misses the name), and, for half a minute after an answer, follow-up remarks that sound meant for it. Other talk at the bench is not sent. The AI service may decide something was not meant for it, and then nothing is said or kept. When the assistant says it cannot tell what it is looking at, the app watches the camera on the phone for up to half a minute and, if the view changes, sends the new picture to the AI service once or twice more without you asking again.
  • The short clips saved with each question are silent.
  • Videos you film yourself for a recap can include sound. They stay on your phone.

Camera and bystanders

  • The camera sees whatever is in front of it, including people.
  • When you ask a question, the pictures in view at that moment are sent to the AI service.
  • Ask people before you point the camera at them.

Your keys

  • Benchside works with API keys from your own accounts: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google for the answers, and optionally Roboflow and ElevenLabs.
  • Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on this phone only. They are readable only while the phone is unlocked, they are not synced to iCloud Keychain, and they are not restored onto another device from a backup.
  • Each key is sent only to the service it belongs to. For Roboflow, that is the endpoint you chose. We never receive your keys.
  • You can remove your keys in Settings.

Purchases

Benchside offers a one-time “Pro” unlock through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We get no card details.

This website

This site and the page a pairing code opens are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub sees your IP address when you load them, as any web host does. The pairing code itself is not sent to the site.

Booklet Builder

When you follow an instruction booklet, you add your own PDF or hold your paper booklet up to the camera. The app does not fetch instructions from any website. If you tap “Find my booklet online”, Safari opens a web search for what you typed. The search provider and the site you download from see that like any visit; Benchside does not fetch the file. A PDF you then share to the app is copied onto this phone only. Pages of a PDF you added, and whatever is in the camera picture, are sent with your questions, as described above.

Recap videos and sharing

A recap video is saved to Photos when you tap Save to Photos or one of the “post to” buttons. Those buttons save the video, then open the other app. They do not post anything for you.

Adults only

Benchside is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. It is not directed to children.

Retention and deletion

  • Your account record, if you made one, is kept until you delete the account (Settings › Account › Delete account), which deletes it straight away. Without an account we hold nothing.
  • On your phone, things stay until you delete them. Deleting a project deletes its photos, clips, recap, instructions and saved questions and answers. You can also clear one project's history on its own, from the History screen or from Settings.
  • Keys are removed in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything except Keychain items (your keys and the free-question count). Remove your keys in Settings first if you want them gone.
  • Data held by the AI service you chose, Roboflow or ElevenLabs is held under your own account with that company. To see or delete it, contact that company. Their privacy policies are linked above.
  • Email you sent us, and your account record if you made one, are the only things we have. We use email to answer you.
