Benchside Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Who we are

Benchside is an iPhone app published by Shimsky Labs LLC, a company in the United States. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Shimsky Labs LLC.

Email: hello@shimskylabs.com
Postal address: 15 Round Hill Drive, Briarcliff Manor, NY 10510, USA

Benchside is a hands-free helper for building things at a bench. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • We do not collect your data. Benchside has no accounts, no sign-in and no server run by us. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The only thing we ever receive from you is email you choose to send us.
  • AI answers use your own key. When you ask a question, the app sends pictures and your question straight from your phone to Anthropic’s AI service, using your own Anthropic API key. It asks your permission once, before the first send.
  • Everything else is optional. Roboflow, ElevenLabs and the downloadable voice are used only if you set them up yourself.
  • Your projects stay on your phone. Notes, photos, clips and recap videos are kept on the phone, and you can delete them.

What stays on your phone

These are kept on your phone only. We cannot see them.

  • Your projects and notes.
  • The questions you asked and the answers you got, kept with each project so you can come back to them or carry on from an earlier point. They can be read only while the phone is unlocked and are left out of iCloud and computer backups.
  • While the camera is open: a photo of the bench about every 45 seconds, and a short silent clip of each question. These are for the build recap. A switch in Settings turns this off.
  • Instruction PDFs you added.
  • Recap videos the app has made, and videos you film for a recap.
  • Your API keys, in the iOS Keychain.
  • A count of the free questions you have used. It is in the iOS Keychain, so it is still there if you delete the app and install it again.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup can include Benchside’s files, as it does for other apps. Backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Second camera

You can pair another phone as a second camera by scanning a QR code. The pairing code is kept in the Keychain on each phone and is never sent over the internet. Its pictures are streamed over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, to the paired phone only. When your recap switch is on, the main phone also keeps those pictures for the recap. They are included with the questions you send to the AI service.

Microphone and speech

  • Benchside uses Apple’s speech framework to turn what you say into text. This happens on your device when your device supports it. Otherwise Apple processes the audio.
  • Benchside never stores or uploads the audio it hears.
  • With hands-free on, the microphone listens while the app is open. Only what you say to the assistant is sent to the AI service, and it is sent as text. That means what follows its name, and, in the two minutes after an answer, questions and remarks that sound meant for it; the AI service may decide one of those was not meant for it, and then nothing is said or kept.
  • The short clips saved with each question are silent.
  • Videos you film yourself for a recap can include sound. They stay on your phone.

Camera and bystanders

  • The camera sees whatever is in front of it, including people.
  • When you ask a question, the pictures in view at that moment are sent to the AI service.
  • Ask people before you point the camera at them.

Your keys

  • Benchside works with API keys from your own accounts: Anthropic, and optionally Roboflow and ElevenLabs.
  • Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on this phone only. They are readable only while the phone is unlocked, they are not synced to iCloud Keychain, and they are not restored onto another device from a backup.
  • Each key is sent only to the service it belongs to. For Roboflow, that is the endpoint you chose. We never receive your keys.
  • You can remove your keys in Settings.

Purchases

Benchside offers a one-time “Pro” unlock through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We get no card details.

This website

This site and the page a pairing code opens are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub sees your IP address when you load them, as any web host does. The pairing code itself is not sent to the site.

Booklet Builder

When you follow an instruction booklet, you add your own PDF or hold your paper booklet up to the camera. The app does not fetch instructions from any website. If you tap “Find my booklet online”, Safari opens a web search for what you typed. The search provider and the site you download from see that like any visit; Benchside does not fetch the file. A PDF you then share to the app is copied onto this phone only. Pages of a PDF you added, and whatever is in the camera picture, are sent with your questions, as described above.

Recap videos and sharing

A recap video is saved to Photos when you tap Save to Photos or one of the “post to” buttons. Those buttons save the video, then open the other app. They do not post anything for you.

Adults only

Benchside is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it. It is not directed to children.

Retention and deletion

  • We hold nothing. Because we hold no user data, we cannot access or delete anything on your behalf.
  • On your phone, things stay until you delete them. Deleting a project deletes its photos, clips, recap, instructions and saved questions and answers. You can also clear one project's history on its own, from the History screen or from Settings.
  • Keys are removed in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything except Keychain items (your keys and the free-question count). Remove your keys in Settings first if you want them gone.
  • Data held by Anthropic, Roboflow or ElevenLabs is held under your own account with that company. To see or delete it, contact that company. Their privacy policies are linked above.
  • Email you sent us is the only thing we have. We use it to answer you.
